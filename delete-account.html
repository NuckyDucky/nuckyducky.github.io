LAST SIGNAL — Delete Your Account

Developer: Nucky Duck Gaming
Contact: nuckyduckgaming@gmail.com

You can delete your LAST SIGNAL account and the data associated with it at any time, either inside the Game or by email.

Option 1 — In the Game

  1. Open LAST SIGNAL.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate. The Game returns to a fresh, signed-out state.

Option 2 — By email

  1. Email nuckyduckgaming@gmail.com with the subject "LAST SIGNAL account deletion".
  2. Include your Player ID (shown in Settings → Account) so we can find the right account. If you signed in with Google Play Games, tell us that too.
  3. We will confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your player account and anonymous player ID
  • Cloud save data and game progress
  • In-game currency balances
  • Your friends list and friend requests
  • The link between the Game and your Google Play Games account (achievements you earned remain in your Google account under Google's control)

What is kept

  • Purchase records are held by Google Play, not by us. Your "Ad Free" purchase stays attached to your Google account and can be restored if you reinstall.
  • Server logs that may reference your former player ID are automatically deleted within 30 days.
  • Your Advertising ID is managed by Android; you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Deletion cannot be undone. See also our Privacy Policy.
