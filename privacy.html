LAST SIGNAL — Privacy Policy

Effective: 28 August 2026 · Developer: Nucky Duck Gaming · Contact: nuckyduckgaming@gmail.com

LAST SIGNAL is developed by Nucky Duck Gaming ("we"). This policy describes what information the game and its closed beta signup page handle and why.

What we collect

Closed beta signup. When you request access to the LAST SIGNAL closed beta through our website, the only information you provide is the Google account email address you intend to use with Google Play. We also record the submission time and an internal processing status. The signup is sent through Google Apps Script and temporarily stored in a private Google Sheet solely so we can add the submitted account to the Google Play closed-test tester list.

We delete the temporary signup row after processing the request. The submitted address remains in the Google Play tester list only while closed-beta access is active and is removed when the closed beta ends, or earlier if you ask us to remove it. We do not use beta signup addresses for newsletters, advertising, or unrelated marketing. We do not sell or publish them.

Game progress. Your profile (stages cleared, upgrades, currencies, settings) is stored locally on your device. If cloud save is enabled, it is also stored with Unity Gaming Services so you can restore it.

Leaderboards. When a run finishes, the game submits your score, your chosen player name, and an anonymous player ID to Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) to operate the Farthest Stage and Daily Challenge leaderboards. Your player name and scores are visible to other players. No email address or real name is collected or shown through the leaderboards.

Purchases. In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The game keeps an anonymous record of completed purchases (order IDs) to deliver what you bought and prevent double-granting.

Advertising. The game shows optional rewarded ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its mediated networks, which may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads as described in their own policies. Buying "Remove Ads" disables all advertising within LAST SIGNAL.

Chat. The optional global chat is operated by Unity Vivox. Messages you send, your player name, and an anonymous player ID are processed by Vivox to deliver chat, are visible to other players, and are filtered by an automated moderation service (Vivox SafeText). You can block other players, report messages (reports are reviewed by us through Unity Moderation), and turn chat off entirely in the Comms screen.

Diagnostics. Crash and usage diagnostics are disabled. The game does not collect analytics about you.

Loot boxes and odds

Some items can be bought with Diamonds, an in-game currency that can be earned by playing or bought with real money. The drop odds of every loot box are displayed in the game before every purchase. Everything sold for Diamonds can also be earned by playing.

Children

LAST SIGNAL and its beta signup page are not intended to solicit personal information from children. Do not submit another person's email address. If you believe a child's address was submitted to the beta signup page, contact us and we will delete any pending signup record and remove the address from the tester list.

Your choices

  • You can withdraw a pending beta request or ask to be removed from the Google Play tester list at any time by contacting us from the submitted address.
  • You can play fully offline; leaderboards, chat, and cloud features simply deactivate.
  • You can turn chat off at any time in the Comms screen and block any player permanently.
  • You can reset your profile from the title screen, which deletes your local progress.
  • To request deletion of leaderboard entries and cloud data, contact us with your in-game player ID (shown on the Account screen): nuckyduckgaming@gmail.com

Third parties

  • Google Apps Script and Google Sheets process and temporarily store closed-beta signup requests; Google Play Console manages the closed-test tester list. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing processes purchases. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Gaming Services provides leaderboards, cloud save, and anonymous authentication. See Unity's Privacy Policy.
  • Unity LevelPlay provides rewarded advertising. See Unity's Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Vivox provides chat, and Unity Moderation handles submitted reports. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

Changes

We will update this page when the game's or beta signup page's data handling changes and adjust the effective date above.
