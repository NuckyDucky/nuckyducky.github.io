LAST SIGNAL — Privacy Policy

Effective: 20 August 2026 · Developer: Nucky Duck Gaming · Contact: nuckyduckgaming@gmail.com

LAST SIGNAL is developed by Nucky Duck Gaming ("we"). This policy describes what information the game handles and why.

What we collect

Game progress. Your profile (stages cleared, upgrades, currencies, settings) is stored locally on your device. If cloud save is enabled, it is also stored with Unity Gaming Services so you can restore it.

Leaderboards. When a run finishes, the game submits your score, your chosen player name, and an anonymous player ID to Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) to operate the Farthest Stage and Daily Challenge leaderboards. Your player name and scores are visible to other players. No email address or real name is collected or shown.

Purchases. In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The game keeps an anonymous record of completed purchases (order IDs) to deliver what you bought and prevent double-granting.

Advertising. The game shows optional rewarded ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its mediated networks, which use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads as described in their own policies. Buying "Remove Ads" disables all advertising.

Chat. The optional global chat is operated by Unity Vivox. Messages you send, your player name, and an anonymous player ID are processed by Vivox to deliver chat, are visible to other players, and are filtered by an automated moderation service (Vivox SafeText). You can block other players, report messages (reports are reviewed by us through Unity Moderation), and turn chat off entirely in the Comms screen.

Diagnostics. Crash and usage diagnostics are disabled. The game does not collect analytics about you.

Loot boxes and odds

Some items can be bought with Diamonds, an in-game currency that can be earned by playing or bought with real money. The drop odds of every loot box are displayed in the game before every purchase. Everything sold for Diamonds can also be earned by playing.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and no data is collected beyond what is described above. Chat messages pass through automated filtering and human-reviewed reports as described above, and chat can be turned off at any time.

Your choices

  • You can play fully offline; leaderboards, chat, and cloud features simply deactivate.
  • You can turn chat off at any time (Comms screen) and block any player permanently.
  • You can reset your profile from the title screen, which deletes your local progress.
  • To request deletion of leaderboard entries and cloud data, contact us with your in-game player ID (shown on the Account screen): nuckyduckgaming@gmail.com

Third parties

  • Google Play Billing (purchases) — see Google Play's privacy policy.
  • Unity Gaming Services: leaderboards, cloud save, authentication (anonymous) — see Unity's privacy policy.
  • Unity LevelPlay (rewarded ads) — see Unity's privacy policy.
  • Unity Vivox (chat) and Unity Moderation (report handling) — see Unity's privacy policy.

Changes

We will update this page when the game's data handling changes and adjust the date above.
